SCP-280 · Dr Gears

Based on SCP-280 by Dr Gears and concepts from the SCP Wiki and its authors, under CC BY-SA 3.0. The directional-light encounter, corridor, dark chamber, external control and pacing are game adaptations. Full relocation and phasing abilities are not simulated. No article photograph or Foundation logo is included.
