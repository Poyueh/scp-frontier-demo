出勤指南

v0.0.4 · 林緣失聯前哨。首個可公開試玩的原型；直接載入遊戲主選單；首區引導與多指觸控已對齊，共用核心流程不另行分叉。手機真機與新玩家盲測尚未完成。

操作

按鍵用途
A / D左右移動。同方向連按兩次並按住可衝刺；耗盡耐力後會喘息再慢慢恢復。
E靠近設備互動。付費工作按住可逐份投入；離開或放開後保留短暫續投時間。等飛行物資抵達再判斷完成。
J / 滑鼠左鍵啟動前哨後使用電擊裝置，對付失控保全機。
K取得脈衝裝置後近身解圍，有冷卻時間。
F切換朝面向的定向照明。
Esc暫停、重新出勤、切換三種語言及查看署名。離開遊戲焦點也會暫停。

手機操作

建議橫向握持。左側左右鍵移動，同方向雙按後按住可衝刺；右側提供攻擊、互動（按住投入）、脈衝與照明，上方暫停。可同時移動與操作；離開按鈕、切換頁面、失焦或旋轉會釋放輸入。手機真機效能與Safari完整實玩尚待驗證。

第一趟出勤

  1. 靠近供能核心，用 E 啟動前哨。
  2. 東側工作台投入兩份物資製作工程工具；待命人員會自行領取。
  3. 委託工程師清障，靠近他收取成果，再在空地投入建造隔離障礙。
  4. 沿東側維修廊道前進；解除失控保全機後，親自領取脈衝裝置。
  5. 回前哨委託物資運輸，或先加固防線。西側有人員可救援。
SCP-280 遭遇提示（含解法）

更深處的黑色異常沒有可刷取的擊殺獎勵。保持距離，朝它開啟定向照明，使它退入東側暗室。待它進入後，回到外側控制柱按 E 恢復隔離。本場景只改編部分條目規則，並非完整設定模擬。

存檔與試玩範圍

每五秒及暫停時自動保存，重新進入可選「繼續旅程」；新出勤建立獨立旅程紀錄，不覆寫既有旅程；主選單「旅程紀錄」可選擇繼續，原有單一紀錄仍可讀取。紀錄保存在同一瀏覽器、同一網站的本機資料中，不會雲端同步；清除網站資料會刪除紀錄。私密瀏覽或停用網站儲存可能無法保存；請留意遊戲內儲存失敗提示。

這版只開放首區，兩端封鎖。已接入多指觸控操作，建議橫向遊玩；尚未完成真機效能驗收。ZIP 請完整解壓後放到網站上遊玩，不能直接雙擊遊戲 HTML。

第一趟出勤

  1. 靠近供能核心，按 E 启动前哨。
  2. 东侧工作台投入两份物资制作工程工具，待命人员自行领取。

SCP-280 提示（含解法）

异常没有击杀奖励。保持距离，面向它开启定向照明，使它退入东侧暗室，再返回外侧控制柱按 E 恢复隔离。这是部分规则的游戏改编，未模拟条目全部能力。

Field guide

v0.0.4 · The Silent Outpost. An early public first-slice prototype. The game now opens directly to its main menu, with aligned first-area guidance and multitouch controls. Mobile-device performance and new-player playtesting remain pending.

Controls

KeyAction
A / DMove. Double-tap a direction and hold to sprint. Exhaustion makes you catch your breath before recovering through slow movement.
EUse nearby equipment. Hold to invest supplies one at a time. A short grace period permits continuing a partial payment; wait for the flying supply to arrive.
J / left clickUse your electric weapon against malfunctioning security units after restoring power.
KUse the recovered pulse device to clear nearby threats; cooldown applies.
FToggle a directed light facing your current direction.
EscPause, start another expedition, change language or read credits. Losing focus also pauses.

Phone controls

Landscape is recommended. Use the left direction controls; double-tap and hold the same direction to sprint. The right controls provide Attack, hold Use to invest, Pulse and Light, with Pause above. Multiple fingers can move and act together. Leaving a button, losing focus or rotating releases input. Physical-device performance and complete mobile Safari playtesting remain unverified.

Your first expedition

  1. Approach the power core and press E to restore the outpost.
  2. Invest two supplies at the eastern workbench. Personnel collect the finished engineering tool themselves.
  3. Commission debris clearance, approach the engineer to collect the output, then build a barrier on the cleared plot.
  4. Follow the eastern corridor. Disable the security units, then deliberately collect the pulse device.
  5. Return to commission supplies or strengthen the perimeter. Survivors remain to the west.
SCP-280 hint (contains solution)

The anomaly has no kill reward. Keep your distance and illuminate it to drive it into the eastern dark chamber. Return to the external shutter control and press E to restore isolation. This scenario adapts only part of the article's rules.

Saving and scope

Progress saves every five seconds and when pausing. Choose Continue expedition on your next visit. Starting a new expedition creates a separate record without replacing an existing run. Expedition records in the main menu lets you resume, including the legacy single checkpoint. Saves belong to this browser and website, with no cloud sync. Clearing website data deletes them; private browsing or blocked storage may prevent saving. Watch for the in-game save failure notice.

Only the first area is open, with sealed endpoints. Multitouch controls are implemented; landscape is recommended and real-device performance remains unverified. Unzip the complete Web archive onto a web server; opening the game HTML directly from disk does not work.
